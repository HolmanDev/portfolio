What is an "Astrobee"?

An Astrobee is a free-flying robot designed to help astronauts with tasks on the International Space Station (ISS). It can move around on its own and its main job is to help the crew with science experiments, monitoring equipment, and other chores, saving the astronauts time to focus on important research.

Purpose of this project

I persued this project to get an understanding of software architectures and general special requirements when working with robotics in space. Since the goal was to obtain generally applicable knowledge, I chose to do a simple movement test with a simulated scan instead of implementing a more complex mission which would have required me to study loads of Astrobee-specific documentation.
